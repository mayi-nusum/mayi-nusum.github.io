Overview

Kasowo is an online accounting service for freelancers and small businesses in Poland. Invoices, taxes and ZUS (social security) live in one place; a dedicated accountant takes care of the rest, and owners see everything as it happens.

What I designed

  • Marketing site and pricing: a clear value proposition and three simple plans.
  • Dashboard: income, costs and profit at a glance, with upcoming tax and ZUS deadlines.
  • Invoices: list, status filters and a fast new-invoice flow with company lookup by tax ID and KSeF e-invoicing.
  • Expenses: drag-and-drop or photo upload of receipts, with categories and review status.
  • Taxes & ZUS: deadlines, amounts and one-click transfers.
  • Accountant: chat and call booking with your own accountant.
  • Mobile app: receipt scanning, reports, contractors, notifications and Polish/English language settings.
