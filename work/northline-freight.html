Overview

Northline Freight moves road, sea and air cargo across Europe and Asia. The goal was a site and customer portal where shippers get a price in seconds, book with confidence and follow every shipment from pickup to proof of delivery.

What I designed

  • Home with instant quote: price a shipment without an account.
  • Services: road, sea, air and warehousing with clear starting prices.
  • Booking flow: route, cargo, options and confirm, with a live shipment summary and transparent pricing.
  • Options: Economy, Standard and Express compared side by side, including delivery windows, insurance and CO₂e.
  • Tracking: route progress, ETA window and a full update timeline.
  • Shipments dashboard: active shipments, on-time rate, invoices and items that need attention.
