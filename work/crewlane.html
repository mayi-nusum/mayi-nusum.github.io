Overview

Crewlane brings payroll, leave, hiring and compliance together for growing Indian companies. Salaries, reimbursements and full-and-final settlements run in one place, while PF, ESI, TDS and professional tax get filed on time.

What I designed

  • Marketing site: a clear, calm pitch with live product previews.
  • Overview dashboard: headcount, open roles, payroll status, approvals waiting and an at-a-glance payroll timeline.
  • People: a searchable directory with filters and rich employee profiles (job, time off, pay, documents).
  • Payroll run: a guided review, adjust, approve and pay flow with pre-checks before money moves.
  • Time off: a team calendar, approval queue and holiday list.
  • Mobile app: run payroll, an inbox for approvals and updates, and personal settings.
