Overview

Field Doctor is a UK meal-subscription service that delivers dietitian-designed, chef-made frozen meals, matched to health needs like gut health, weight management and heart health. As part of a team of 4, I worked on the UI/UX of its responsive website in 2024.

The aim was to help first-time visitors understand the offer quickly, find meals that fit their health goals, and manage a flexible plan with confidence.

The challenge

Turn a complex, health-led product into something simple to choose, trust and manage.

  • A big menu, one person's needs: 60+ meals, 16 ranges and 14 allergen filters.
  • Health claims need trust: nutrition facts, allergens and expert backing must be clear.
  • Flexible, not locked-in: plan, one-off, skip, pause. The model must feel simple and low-risk.

My work

Site audit, audience and journey mapping, information architecture, wireframes, a design system, and the final desktop and mobile UI for the home page, personalisation quiz, menu and filters, meal detail, plan management and health hub.
