Zomato: Excess Food Supply Management

A platform concept that connects restaurants with surplus food to NGOs, food banks and delivery partners.

Role
UX Designer
Team
8 members · Bengaluru
Duration
Aug 2024 – Apr 2025
Platforms

Tools

Overview

Designing a simpler way for surplus restaurant food to reach NGOs and food banks before it goes to waste. I worked in a team of 8 alongside Zomato product managers, developers and business stakeholders.

My responsibilities: research with restaurants, NGOs, delivery partners and food banks; personas and journeys for every stakeholder; low to high fidelity wireframes and prototypes for mobile and web; tracking UX metrics; and aligning design with product, tech and business teams.

The problem

Surplus food exists. Getting it to people in time is the hard part.

Restaurants only know about surplus at closing, when staff are busiest. NGOs need reliable, early information to plan distribution. Delivery partners can carry food, but pickups are unclear and unplanned. Today, redistribution depends on phone calls, messages and goodwill.

Research

Four stakeholder groups and five methods: interviews, surveys, observational studies at closing time, competitive analysis and card sorting. Affinity mapping grouped the findings into four themes: timing, trust, coordination and effort.

Key insight: donating must take seconds, and every handover must be confirmed.

Outcome

Prototypes for mobile and web were tested with each stakeholder group against three UX metrics: task completion rate, time to complete a request and error rate.

  • Design for the busiest moment of the day, not the average one.
  • Trust is a feature: show safety, status and proof at every step.
  • Four stakeholder groups need one shared source of truth.
